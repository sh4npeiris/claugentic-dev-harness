## On the doc-budget question (for the record)

The diagnostic examined the harness's own doc-budgets directly. **Verdict: the ledger-only scope is correct** — the 12 KB ROADMAP / 40 KB DECISIONS / 6 KB CLAUDE.md caps guard the three *accumulating* ledgers, not capability; the large *content* docs (`security.md` 33 KB, `WORKFLOW` 27 KB) are deliberately uncapped, and capping them was recommended **against** (DIAG-61 — it would pressure under-documenting the bar). The 450-char limit is per index *line* (a one-liner forcing-function), not per file. Real refinements surfaced: a ~90% **WARN band** before DECISIONS breaks the build (DIAG-63); a note that **bytes are a cheap proxy for tokens** (DIAG-64); consolidate the honesty-split now **restated across 9 docs** (DIAG-65). This very backlog (~20 KB) lives in its own doc precisely because of the digest/detail split the budget is built around.
